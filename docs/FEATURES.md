## Before this goes public

The pitch text contains claims a viewer may read as promises. The demo avoids them, and each needs evidence or softer wording before launch:

- "Guarantee AI won't take your job" and "immediate replacement to traditional employment"
- "Valuations up to 100m"
- "Be rich much faster" and fast returns
- "Powered by OpenAI/ChatGPT Astra 6" (needs the provider's permission and a verified product name)
- "No product like it exists yet"

Income examples in the demo are labelled sample data. Use real, receipted numbers when they exist.
